## The drive is the first reason to come south

Girdwood is 40 miles from Anchorage down the Seward Highway — about forty-five minutes, along the edge of Turnagain Arm, on a road that is a destination in its own right. The Knik River valley is an hour north on the Old Glenn. If your day already involves Alyeska, Portage or the drive toward Seward, the southern flights fit and the northern ones do not.

All three Girdwood tours leave from the Alpine Air Alaska hangar at Girdwood Airport, 599 Mt. Hood Drive. Round-trip transport from Anchorage is not included; Alpine Air runs a shuttle from Anchorage hotels and the airport for $75 per person round trip.

## Three tours, three quite different purchases

**{{< gyg-link tour="1107685" >}}Girdwood: Chugach Mountains Helicopter Flight with Landing{{< /gyg-link >}}** — the simple one. A one-hour flight into the Chugach backcountry and one glacier landing, with fifteen to twenty minutes on foot on the ice. In spring and summer the glaciers out here carry blue meltwater pools too. From $620, rated 4.9 from 14 guests. It is the cheapest way onto a glacier from Girdwood, though still about $100 more than the Knik valley landing tours, which also give more time on the ice.

**[90-min Flight with 2 Landings](/tours/anchorage-90-min-flight-with-2-landings/)** — the longest flight on this site. Ninety minutes through the Chugach with **two landings**: one on glacier ice, where you get around thirty minutes, and one on a high alpine ridge or a remote mountain lake. That second stop is the reason to book it. A glacier landing gives you ice; a ridge landing gives you the view back across the icefield you have just flown over, which is a different and often better photograph. From $844 via {{< gyg-link tour="1078337" >}}Anchorage: 90-min Flight with 2 Landings{{< /gyg-link >}}.

Note the **two-passenger minimum**. Solo travellers cannot simply book online — call the operator, who will tell you which departures already have someone on them. Arrive fifteen minutes early for the briefing.

**[Helicopter Glacier Dogsledding Tour](/tours/anchorage-helicopter-glacier-dogsledding-tour/)** — flies up to Punchbowl Glacier, a snowfield at around 3,200 feet, where Mitch Seavey's IdidaRide kennel keeps a summer camp. Roughly 85 minutes on the snow: a sled run with a musher guide, a tour of the camp, and time with the dogs and the summer puppies. Outer layers and boots are provided at the hangar, so you do not need to arrive dressed for a glacier. Groups capped at ten. From $769 through {{< gyg-link tour="1079145" >}}Anchorage: Helicopter Glacier Dogsledding Tour{{< /gyg-link >}}.

## What Girdwood does not give you

Bare blue ice. Punchbowl is a high snowfield that holds snow year-round — that is exactly why a dog team can live on it in July — so what you land on is white rather than the crevassed, meltwater-pooled surface that makes [Knik Glacier](/knik-glacier-helicopter-tours/) photograph the way it does. The 90-minute flight's glacier landing does put you on ice, but the route and the terrain are alpine Chugach rather than a broad valley glacier.

It also does not give you the cheapest option. The lowest Girdwood price is $620, for fifteen to twenty minutes on the ice; the Knik valley starts at $520 for a landing with forty-five minutes on it. If cost is the deciding factor, drive north.

## Weather: the honest caveat

Turnagain Arm pulls in coastal cloud. The operators flying from the Knik River valley claim a better dispatch rate than glacier flights working out of Girdwood, Seward, Juneau, Haines or Skagway — a marketing claim rather than a published statistic, but the geography behind it is real, since the inland valley sits further back from the water.

That does not make Girdwood a bad bet. Girdwood is a much wetter place, with more than five times Palmer's yearly precipitation in NOAA's normals, so schedule the flight early in your trip, and every tour carries free cancellation up to 24 hours before departure. If your trip contains exactly one flyable window and you cannot afford to be rescheduled, the [northern flights](/knik-glacier-helicopter-tours/) are the safer choice. The [best-time guide](/guides/best-time-for-an-anchorage-glacier-helicopter-tour/) covers the seasonal picture.
