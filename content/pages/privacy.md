## Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 2026

This website ("we", "us", "our") operates as a GetYourGuide affiliate booking platform. This page explains how we handle your personal data.

### Important: Data Processing

**Booking data** (name, payment details, contact info) is collected and processed by **GetYourGuide**, not by this website. When you make a booking, you are providing your information directly to GetYourGuide under their privacy policy.

**Please review:** [GetYourGuide Privacy Policy](https://www.getyourguide.com/privacy)

### Information We Collect

This website collects minimal information:

- **Website analytics:** We use Google Analytics (with IP anonymization) to understand how visitors use our site. This includes pages viewed, time on site, and general location (city/country level).
- **Contact form data:** If you email us directly, we collect your email address and message content to respond to your inquiry.

We **do not** collect, store, or process:
- Payment information
- Booking details
- Passport or ID numbers
- Precise location data

### How We Use Your Information

- To respond to customer service inquiries sent via email
- To improve website content and user experience
- To analyze website traffic patterns (anonymized)

### Cookies

We use cookies for:
- Google Analytics (anonymized traffic analysis)
- Remembering user preferences (e.g., language selection)

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. This may affect some website functionality.

### Third-Party Services

- **GetYourGuide:** Processes all booking transactions. Review their [privacy policy](https://www.getyourguide.com/privacy).
- **Google Analytics:** Tracks anonymized website usage. Review their [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Your Rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR, you have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Object to data processing
- Data portability

**For booking data:** Contact GetYourGuide customer service.

**For website contact data:** Email us at {{< param contactEmail >}}.

### Data Retention

- Analytics data: Retained for 26 months (Google Analytics default)
- Email correspondence: Retained for 2 years or until deletion requested

### Children's Privacy

This website does not knowingly collect data from children under 16. Bookings must be made by adults.

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
